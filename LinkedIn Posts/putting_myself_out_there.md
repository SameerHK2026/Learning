# Putting Myself Out There

Since I started career coaching, I have had to do a lot of things that exposed me.

The older version of me would not have done these things.

One of them is posting here on LinkedIn regularly.

Opening my thoughts to others was scary!

I had never done any sort of marketing or sales activities in my prior roles. To tell someone about my own coaching services caused me anxiety, despite it being the only way to begin coaching.

It still causes some anxiety before publishing my work, talking about my services, or asking for fees for my services.

## The Lesson

One of the most important lessons I have learnt during the last 15 months is to put myself out there.

Any activity I choose, exposing myself has been the only way to learn.

- Opting to speak in public.
- Delivering a session to students.
- Cold calling clients.
- Visiting a client location and asking for a meeting.

All these have given me immense experience in getting out of my shell that I thought was protecting me.

It was constraining me.

## Sharing Something Personal

This past Saturday, I shared something that I have been doing with a group of close friends.

Even then, it was scary!

My voice was shaking.

I was shivering internally.

To stand (was sitting, actually) in front of people, when everyone is watching you, and share something you have guarded is extremely scary.

It's akin to giving a public performance.

I had read about great artists who would become nervous before a public performance.

## How Do I Develop This Skill?

Get into more opportunities. More scenarios in which I am exposed, where I don't have a place to hide but to stand and deliver.

This feels like learning to drive.

Scary in the beginning.

Smooth with enough practice.

It may still make me nervous.

But now I know this is the only way.

> To keep putting myself out there.
